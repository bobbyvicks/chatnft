How it works

1. Design it

Pick a painted or stained base, a pattern, your words in your font, and your logo. What you see is what we print.

2. We build it

Regulation 24" x 48" boards, 3/4" Baltic birch tops on a solid frame, folding legs, 6" hole 9" from the top. Your design is printed and sealed under a clear coat.

3. It ships

Built to order in about three weeks (or five business days with the rush option) and shipped freight to your door.
