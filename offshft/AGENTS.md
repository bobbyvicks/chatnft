# Rules for agents working on OFF SHFT

Read README.md and BRAND.md before doing anything.

## You can do without asking

- Draft designs, mockups, listings, page copy and social posts in this repo
- Research trends, competitors and keywords
- Update status columns in DESIGNS.md and checklists in LAUNCH_PLAN.md
- Write the weekly report in `reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

## Ask the owner first, every time

- Spending any money (ads, samples, domains, apps, digitization fees)
- Publishing or unpublishing a product, or changing a live price
- Posting anything publicly or messaging customers
- Changing store settings, payments, shipping or tax
- Using any phrase, logo or artwork you haven't checked for existing trademarks

## Never

- Copy someone else's artwork, logo or slogan
- Use sports team, league, brand or golf course names or logos
- Make claims about the product that aren't true (materials, "made in Canada", etc.)
- Create fake reviews, fake scarcity or fake discounts

## How to hand off work

- One folder per design in `designs/NN-name/` with `design.svg`, `mockup.png` and `notes.md`
- Open a pull request for each batch of work with a short summary and what you need the owner to decide
