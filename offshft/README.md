# OFF SHFT

Hats for the hours you earned. An outdoor-hobby headwear brand for people who work hard and play on their time off.

- **Site:** offshft.com (Shopify)
- **Products:** embroidered and printed hats, made and shipped by a print-on-demand partner (Printful or Printify)
- **Audience:** people who work hard and spend their time off fishing, golfing, playing cornhole, and at the cottage or backyard

**Owner (Robert):** owns every account, approves anything that spends money, publishes products, posts to social.

**Agents:** create designs, write listings, draft social posts, research trends, report numbers. Agents propose; the owner approves.
